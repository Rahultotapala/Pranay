How to play

↔Steer left/right
Keep your hand centered and tilt your wrist like a steering wheel. A small flat-hand deadzone keeps the runner steady.
↑Catch fruit
Pinch to jump into apples, oranges, bananas, and strawberries.
🤏Quick pinch
Touch your index finger to your thumb for an instant jump. No arm movement needed.
✋Open palm
Spread all five fingers wide to activate the shield.
●Closed fist
Make a fist to smash purple sigil obstacles.
2Co-op mode
Turn on Co-op and use one hand per Bubu. Both players use the same gestures and must survive.
